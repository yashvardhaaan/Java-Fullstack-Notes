# Why Learn Prepositions?

Prepositions connect nouns or pronouns with other words in a sentence.

A small mistake in a preposition can completely change the meaning.

Example

❌ Incorrect

```
He is married with her.
```

✅ Correct

```
He is married to her.
```

Prepositions are one of the most common topics in

- Error Spotting
- Fill in the Blanks
- Sentence Correction
- Reading Comprehension

---

# What is a Preposition?

A **Preposition** is a word placed before a noun or pronoun to show its relationship with another word.

It usually shows

- Place
- Time
- Direction
- Movement
- Cause
- Manner

---

# Examples

```
The book is on the table.

She arrived at 9 AM.

The cat is under the chair.
```

# 1. Prepositions of Time

Tell **when** something happens.

---

## At

Used for a **specific time**.

Examples

```
At 8 AM

At noon

At midnight

At sunrise
```

---

## On

Used for

- Days
- Dates

Examples

```
On Monday

On Sunday

On 15 August

On my birthday
```

---

## In

Used for

- Months
- Years
- Seasons
- Centuries
- Long periods

Examples

```
In July

In 2026

In winter

In the morning

In the 21st century
```

---

# Time Summary

| Preposition | Used With |
|-------------|-----------|
| At | Exact Time |
| On | Days & Dates |
| In | Months, Years, Seasons |

---

# 2. Prepositions of Place

Show **where** something is.

---

## In

Inside something.

```
In the room

In the box

In India
```

---

## On

Touching a surface.

```
On the table

On the wall

On the floor
```

---

## At

Specific location.

```
At the station

At school

At home

At the office
```

---

## Under

```
Under the bed

Under the bridge
```

---

## Over

```
The fan is over the table.
```

---

## Above

Higher than something.

No direct contact.

---

## Below

Lower than something.

---

## Between

Used for **two** persons or things.

```
Between Rahul and Amit.
```

---

## Among

Used for **more than two**.

```
Among the students.
```

# 3. Prepositions of Direction

Show the direction of movement.

---

## To

```
Go to school.

Walk to the market.
```

---

## Towards

```
She walked towards me.
```

---

## Into

Movement from outside to inside.

```
He entered into the room.
```

(Note: In many contexts, "entered the room" is more natural than "entered into the room.")

---

## Onto

Movement to a surface.

```
The cat jumped onto the table.
```

---

# 6. Preposition of Instrument

Examples

```

```

---

Examples

```

```

# 7. Preposition of Cause

Examples

```
Because of

Due to

Owing to
```

---

Example Sentences

```

```

---

# 8. Compound Prepositions

Made of more than one word.

Examples

```
According to

Instead of

In front of

Because of

Next to

Apart from
```

---

Example

```
According to the report,

sales increased.
```

# Commonly Confused Prepositions

---

## In vs Into

### In

Shows position.

```
The keys are in the bag.
```

---

### Into

Shows movement.

```
She walked into the room.
```

---

## On vs Onto

### On

Position.

```
The book is on the table.
```

---

### Onto

Movement.

```
Put the book onto the table.
```

---

## Between vs Among

### Between

```
Two people/things
```

---

### Among

```
More than two
```

---

## Beside vs Besides

### Beside

Means

```
Next to
```

Example

```
He sat beside me.
```

---

### Besides

Means

```
In addition to
```

Example

```
Besides English,

she knows French.
```

---

## Since vs From

### Since

Used with Present Perfect.

```
I have lived here since 2020.
```

---

### From

General starting point.

```
The office is open from Monday.
```

# Common GATE & Placement Errors

### Incorrect

```
She is married with him.
```

Correct

```
She is married to him.
```

---

### Incorrect

```
He is good in Mathematics.
```

Correct

```
He is good at Mathematics.
```

---

### Incorrect

```
She is interested for music.
```

Correct

```
She is interested in music.
```

---

### Incorrect

```
He discussed about the issue.
```

Correct

```
He discussed the issue.
```

("Discuss" does not take **about**.)

---

### Incorrect

```
She entered into the room.
```

Better

```
She entered the room.
```

# Interview Corner

### Q1. What is a preposition?

A preposition is a word that shows the relationship between a noun or pronoun and another word in a sentence.

---

### Q2. What is the difference between **In** and **Into**?

- **In** shows position.
- **Into** shows movement from outside to inside.

Example

```
The phone is in the bag.

She put the phone into the bag.
```

---

### Q3. What is the difference between **Between** and **Among**?

- **Between** is generally used for two people or things.
- **Among** is used for more than two people or things.
