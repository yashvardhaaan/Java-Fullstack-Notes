# Why Learn Articles?

Articles are small words, but they play a very important role in English.

They tell us whether we are talking about

- A general thing
- A specific thing
- One of many things

Incorrect article usage is one of the most common grammar mistakes in competitive exams.

Example

❌ Incorrect

```
Sun rises in east.
```

✅ Correct

```
The sun rises in the east.
```

---

# What are Articles?

Articles are words placed **before nouns**.

There are **three articles** in English.

```
Articles
│
├── A
├── An
└── The
```

They are divided into two categories.

```
Articles
│
├── Indefinite Articles
│   ├── A
│   └── An
│
└── Definite Article
    └── The
```

---

# Indefinite Articles

Used when referring to **any one** person or thing.

They do **not** refer to something specific.

```
A

An
```

---

# Article "A"

## Rule

Use **A** before words that begin with a **consonant sound**.

> It depends on **sound**, not spelling.

---

## Examples

```
A boy

A car

A university

A European country

A one-time password
```

Notice

```
University

↓

Starts with letter U

But sound is

"Yu"

↓

Consonant sound

↓

Use A
```

---

# Article "An"

## Rule

Use **An** before words that begin with a **vowel sound**.

Again,

It depends on pronunciation.

---

## Examples

```
An apple

An orange

An engineer

An hour

An honest person
```

Notice

```
Hour

↓

H is silent

↓

Starts with vowel sound

↓

Use An
```

---

# A vs An

| Use A | Use An |
|--------|---------|
| A cat | An elephant |
| A pen | An umbrella |
| A university | An hour |
| A European | An honest man |
| A useful idea | An MBA graduate |

---

# Definite Article "The"

Use **The** when talking about a **specific** person, place, or thing.

---

## Examples

```
The book on the table.

The teacher who taught us yesterday.

The Taj Mahal.
```

---


# Special Rules

## Rule 1

Use **The** before superlatives.

```
The best player

The highest mountain
```

---

## Rule 2

Use **The** before ordinal numbers.

```
The first chapter

The second prize
```

---

## Rule 3

Use **A** when mentioning something for the first time.

```
I saw a dog.

The dog was barking.
```

---

## Rule 4

Use **An** only if the pronunciation begins with a vowel sound.

Examples

```
An MBA

An hour

An honest officer
```

# Interview Corner

### Q1. How many articles are there in English?

There are **three** articles:

- A
- An
- The

---

### Q2. What is the difference between **A** and **An**?

- **A** is used before **consonant sounds**.
- **An** is used before **vowel sounds**.

Examples

```
A university

An umbrella
```

---

### Q3. Why do we say "an hour" but "a university"?

Because article usage depends on **pronunciation**, not spelling.

- **Hour** starts with a vowel sound (silent H) → **An**
- **University** starts with a consonant sound ("Yu") → **A**
