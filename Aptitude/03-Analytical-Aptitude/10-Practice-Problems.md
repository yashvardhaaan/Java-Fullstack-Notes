# Common Mistakes

- Ignoring keywords like **immediately**, **only**, and **all**.
- Solving mentally instead of drawing diagrams.
- Forgetting alphabet positions in coding questions.
- Assuming relationships not stated in blood relation problems.
- Confusing left/right in circular arrangements.
- Missing the underlying mathematical pattern in series questions.

---

# Solving Strategy

```
Read Carefully
       │
       ▼
Identify Topic
       │
       ▼
Apply Relevant Rule
       │
       ▼
Eliminate Wrong Options
       │
       ▼
Verify Answer
```

# Quick Revision Sheet

## Logic

- Read every statement carefully.
- Do not make assumptions.

---

## Analogy

- Find the relationship first.
- Apply the same relationship.

---

## Series

Check:

- Addition
- Subtraction
- Multiplication
- Division
- Squares
- Cubes
- Prime Numbers

---

## Odd One Out

Identify the common property shared by most items.

---

## Coding-Decoding

Check in this order:

- +1 / -1 Shift
- +2 / -2 Shift
- Reverse
- Alphabet Position
- Opposite Alphabet

---

## Blood Relations

- Mark gender.
- Draw a family tree.
- Solve one statement at a time.

---

## Directions

- Draw the path.
- Track every turn.
- Use coordinates for distance.

---

## Seating Arrangement

- Read all clues first.
- Place fixed positions.
- Verify every clue.
