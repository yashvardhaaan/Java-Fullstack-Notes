# Series

## Definition

A **Series** is an ordered sequence of numbers, letters, symbols, or figures that follows a specific logical pattern.

The objective is to identify the pattern and determine the **next term**, **missing term**, or **incorrect term**.

General Structure

```
Given Series

↓

Identify Pattern

↓

Apply Rule

↓

Find Answer
```

Example

```
2, 4, 6, 8, ?

Answer

10
```

Pattern

```
+2
```

---

# Why Learn This?

Series recognition is widely used in identifying patterns and making predictions.

Applications include:

- **Artificial Intelligence** – Pattern recognition and machine learning.
- **Software Engineering** – Algorithm design and sequence generation.
- **Data Science** – Time-series analysis and forecasting.
- **Finance** – Stock market trend analysis.
- **Business Analytics** – Sales and demand forecasting.
- **Scientific Research** – Experimental data analysis.

# GATE 80/20 Summary

✅ Solve series in this order:

1. Addition/Subtraction
2. Multiplication/Division
3. First Differences
4. Second Differences
5. Squares
6. Cubes
7. Prime Numbers
8. Fibonacci
9. Alternating Patterns
10. Alphabet Positions

✅ Memorize:

- Squares (1–30)
- Cubes (1–15)
- Alphabet positions (A–Z)
- Prime numbers (up to 100)

✅ Most series questions become easy once you identify the underlying mathematical or alphabetical pattern.
