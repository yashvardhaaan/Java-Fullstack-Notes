# Numerical Reasoning

## Definition

**Numerical Reasoning** is the ability to understand, analyze, and solve problems involving **numbers, mathematical relationships, data, and logical calculations**.

It tests your ability to:

- Identify patterns
- Perform calculations
- Interpret numerical information
- Draw conclusions using mathematical logic

General Structure

```
Given Numbers

↓

Identify Pattern

↓

Apply Mathematical Rule

↓

Find Answer
```

---

# Why Learn This?

Numerical reasoning is used whenever decisions are based on quantitative information.

Applications include:

- **Software Engineering** – Performance analysis and algorithm optimization.
- **Data Science** – Data interpretation and statistical analysis.
- **Finance** – Budgeting, forecasting, and investment analysis.
- **Business Analytics** – KPI tracking and decision-making.
- **Healthcare** – Medical statistics and dosage calculations.
- **Manufacturing** – Production planning and quality control.
