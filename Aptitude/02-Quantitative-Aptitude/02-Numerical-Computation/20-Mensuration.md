# Mensuration

## Definition

**Mensuration** is the branch of mathematics that deals with the **measurement of length, area, perimeter, surface area, and volume** of geometric figures.

It helps calculate the dimensions of two-dimensional (2D) and three-dimensional (3D) shapes.

---

# Why Learn This?

Mensuration is widely used in measuring physical spaces and objects.

Applications include:

- **Civil Engineering** – Estimating construction materials.
- **Architecture** – Designing buildings and floor plans.
- **Manufacturing** – Calculating material requirements.
- **Software Engineering** – Computer graphics and 3D modeling.
- **Interior Design** – Flooring, painting, and furnishing.
- **Agriculture** – Measuring land area and storage capacity.

# Basic Terms

## Perimeter

The total length of the boundary of a closed figure.

Example

```
Rectangle

Perimeter

=

Sum of all sides
```

---

## Area

The amount of surface enclosed by a figure.

Measured in

```
Square Units
```

Example

```
m²

cm²
```

---

## Surface Area

The total area of all faces of a three-dimensional object.

---

## Volume

The amount of space occupied by a solid object.

Measured in

```
Cubic Units
```

Example

```
m³

cm³
```

# GATE 80/20 Summary

✅ Classify the figure first:

- **2D → Area / Perimeter**
- **3D → Surface Area / Volume**

✅ Memorize these high-frequency formulas:

- **Rectangle Area = l × b**
- **Triangle Area = ½ × b × h**
- **Circle Area = πr²**
- **Circle Circumference = 2πr**
- **Cube Volume = a³**
- **Cuboid Volume = l × b × h**
- **Cylinder Volume = πr²h**
- **Cone Volume = ⅓πr²h**
- **Sphere Volume = ⁴⁄₃πr³**

✅ Always check whether the given measurement is **radius or diameter**.

✅ Remember:
- **Area → square units**
- **Volume → cubic units**
